## 2. Fluidic Impaction Physics (Aerosol Separation)

Non-volatile narcotic molecules travel inside exhaled aerosol micro-droplets. To separate droplets from exhaled air without clogging, breath passes through a converging nozzle towards an impaction plate.

### 2.1 Stokes Number ($Stk$)
The particle trajectory is governed by the dimensionless Stokes number:
$$Stk = \frac{\rho_p \cdot d_p^2 \cdot U \cdot C_c}{9 \cdot \mu_{\text{air}} \cdot D_{\text{nozzle}}}$$

Where:
* $\rho_p = 1000\text{ kg/m}^3$: Density of exhaled airway lining fluid droplets.
* $d_p \in [0.1, 5.0]\,\mu\text{m}$: Droplet aerodynamic diameter.
* $U \ge 15.0\text{ m/s}$: Linear nozzle exit velocity generated by human exhalation.
* $C_c \approx 1 + \frac{2\lambda}{d_p} \left(1.257 + 0.4e^{-1.1d_p/2\lambda}\right)$: Cunningham slip correction factor.
* $\mu_{\text{air}} = 1.81 \times 10^{-5}\text{ Pa}\cdot\text{s}$: Dynamic viscosity of air at $20^\circ\text{C}$.
* $D_{\text{nozzle}} = 2.0\text{ mm}$: Nozzle internal diameter.

### 2.2 Cutoff Droplet Diameter ($d_{50}$)
The critical droplet diameter with $50\%$ collection efficiency ($Stk_{50} \approx 0.24$) is:
$$d_{50} = \sqrt{\frac{9 \cdot \mu_{\text{air}} \cdot D_{\text{nozzle}} \cdot Stk_{50}}{\rho_p \cdot U \cdot C_c}} \approx 1.2\,\mu\text{m}$$

All droplets with $d_p > 1.2\,\mu\text{m}$ impact the wetted wicking membrane, while gaseous breath components ($N_2, O_2, CO_2$, volatile gases) follow fluid streamlines into the secondary BME688 matrix chamber.

## 3. Electrochemical Differential Pulse Voltammetry (DPV)

### 3.1 Randles-Ševčík Peak Oxidation Current
For a planar carbon working electrode, the peak Faradaic oxidation current $I_p$ is expressed as:
$$I_p = 0.4463 \cdot n F A C^* \sqrt{\frac{n F v D}{R T}}$$

Where:
* $n = 2$: Two-electron oxidation mechanism (e.g., $\Delta^9$-THC phenolic group oxidizes to corresponding quinone).
* $F = 96,485\text{ C/mol}$: Faraday constant.
* $A = 0.126\text{ cm}^2$: Active surface area of standard $4\text{ mm}$ screen-printed carbon disk.
* $C^*$: Analyte concentration in physiological electrolyte solution ($\text{mol/cm}^3$).
* $v = 50\text{ mV/s}$: Voltammetric potential sweep rate.
* $D = 5.2 \times 10^{-6}\text{ cm}^2/\text{s}$: Analyte diffusion coefficient in aqueous buffer.
* $R = 8.314\text{ J/(mol}\cdot\text{K)}$: Universal gas constant.
* $T = 298.15\text{ K}$: Operating temperature ($25^\circ\text{C}$).

### 3.2 Total Faradaic Charge ($Q$)
The integrated charge over the oxidation potential window $[E_1, E_2]$ is given by:
$$Q = \int_{t(E_1)}^{t(E_2)} I_{\text{Faradaic}}(t) \, dt = \frac{1}{v} \int_{E_1}^{E_2} \left[ I_{\text{measured}}(E) - I_{\text{baseline}}(E) \right] dE$$

## 4. Digital Signal Processing & Baseline Correction

### 4.1 Asymmetric Least Squares (ALS) Baseline Smoothing
To remove non-Faradaic capacitive current drift without corrupting peak height:
$$\min_{\mathbf{z}} \sum_{i=1}^M w_i (y_i - z_i)^2 + \lambda \sum_{i=2}^{M-1} (z_{i+1} - 2z_i + z_{i-1})^2$$

Where the weighting factors $w_i$ enforce baseline adherence:
$$w_i = \begin{cases} 
p, & \text{if } y_i > z_i \\ 
1 - p, & \text{if } y_i \le z_i 
\end{cases} \quad (\text{with } p = 0.001, \; \lambda = 10^5)$$

## 5. 3-Tier Decision Engine Formulation

Let the multimodal feature vector be:
$$\mathbf{X} = \left[ E_p, \; I_p, \; Q, \; t_{\text{breath}}, \; P_{\text{flow}}, \; \Delta R_{\text{gas}} \right]^T \in \mathbb{R}^6$$

1. **Tier 1 (Breath Quality Gate):**
   $$G_1 = \mathbb{I}(t_{\text{breath}} \ge 2.5\text{ s}) \cdot \mathbb{I}(P_{\text{flow}} \ge 0.8\text{ kPa}) \cdot \mathbb{I}(\text{RH} \ge 90\%)$$

2. **Tier 2 (Matrix & Solvent Rejection Gate):**
   $$G_2 = \mathbb{I}(\text{VOC}_{\text{interferent}} < 45\text{ ppm})$$

3. **Tier 3 (TinyML Impairment Decision):**
   $$P(\text{Impairment} \mid \mathbf{X}) = \frac{1}{1 + \exp\left(-\mathbf{w}^T \mathbf{X} - b\right)}$$

$$\text{Final Verdict} = \begin{cases}
\text{INCONCLUSIVE (Retest Required)}, & \text{if } G_1 = 0 \lor G_2 = 0 \lor (0.15 < P < 0.85) \\
\text{POSITIVE}, & \text{if } G_1 = 1 \land G_2 = 1 \land P \ge 0.85 \land E_p \in [E_{\text{window}}] \\
\text{NEGATIVE}, & \text{if } G_1 = 1 \land G_2 = 1 \land P \le 0.15
\end{cases}$$
